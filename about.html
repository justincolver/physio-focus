HELLO, I’M KARLENE

Helping you bridge the gap between recovery and real life.

I’m Karlene — usually Kaz — a specialist musculoskeletal physiotherapist with 16 years’ NHS experience and over a decade teaching Reformer and Mat Pilates.

Throughout my career, I’ve seen how often people finish physiotherapy feeling better, but not yet ready to return to the gym, a class, sport or exercise by themselves. I created The Physio Focus to support people through that important next stage.

My approach combines clinical understanding with progressive, practical exercise. You’ll be listened to, appropriately challenged and given the knowledge to make decisions about your own body with confidence.
